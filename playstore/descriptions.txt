APP NAME (max 30 characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
MYN The Seller


SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Run your shop on MYN: orders, stock, menu and earnings in one place.


FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
MYN The Seller is the shop owner's app for the MYN marketplace. Track
every order, keep your stock current and watch your earnings without
leaving the counter.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Orders at a glance
See what has come in, what is still open and what has been delivered.
Filter by today, yesterday, the last week, month or year, and open any
order to see the full payment breakdown - what the customer paid, tax,
commission, delivery charge and exactly what you earn.

Mark orders packed
New orders sit on your dashboard. One tap moves an order along and it
leaves the list, so the dashboard always shows only what still needs you.

Scan your menu
Photograph your printed menu and the app reads every dish and price off
it. Check the list, correct anything that was misread, and add it all to
your stock in one go - instead of typing forty items by hand.

Manage your items
Edit a dish the same way you would on the web panel: picture, name,
category, food type, packaging charge, status, and as many portion sizes
and prices as you need.

Open and closed
Flip your shop open or closed from the dashboard whenever you need to
stop taking orders.

Your earnings
Total collected and net earnings are on the dashboard, with tax and
commission shown for every single order.

WHO IT IS FOR
Restaurants, bakeries, hypermarkets and any other shop selling on MYN.
You will need a MYN seller account to sign in.

AndroidManifest.xml. Nothing in the app ever requested them
